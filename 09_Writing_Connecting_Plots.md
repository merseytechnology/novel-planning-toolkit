> **Worth remembering**
> A subplot is a small story with its own setup, complication, and resolution — not a scene that happens to feature a different character. If it can't be summarized the way you'd summarize the main plot, it isn't finished yet.

## Weaving checklist

- [ ] Every subplot touches the protagonist's WANT or NEED, directly or in contrast.
- [ ] Each subplot has its own arc — a beginning, a complication, and a resolution.
- [ ] Subplots resolve at or shortly before the climax, not long after it.
- [ ] No major new character enters after the midpoint without a strong reason.
- [ ] Each subplot's theme rhymes with, or deliberately contrasts, the main theme.
- [ ] Cutting any single subplot would leave a visible gap in the main plot.
