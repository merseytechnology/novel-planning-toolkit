# 8. Disconnected Plots

> The parking lot — plot threads, scene ideas, and "what if" fragments that don't connect to your main story yet. Capture them here instead of forcing them in too early.
>
> > **How to use this template**
> > > Not every idea needs a home right away. This page exists so a good fragment doesn't get lost, and so a weak one doesn't get forced into the main plot just because you thought of it. Revisit it when you plan your subplots in [Template 9](09_Writing_Connecting_Plots.md).

> > > ## Questions worth asking before integrating one
> > >
> > > - Does this touch my protagonist's WANT or NEED — directly or by contrast?
> > > - - Does it introduce a character or world rule I'd need to set up much earlier?
> > >   - - Am I keeping this because it serves the story, or because I like it?
> > >     - - Could it survive as a short story instead — and free this novel of it?
